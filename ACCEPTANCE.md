# Buoy acceptance

Target edition: 17 October 2026. Each item is the acceptance evidence for the same-named clause in [CONTRACT.md](CONTRACT.md), run through a public door (the product UI, HTTP API, `rer` commands, a connector against a provider sandbox, or the built site), with the clause's current status: `done`, `partial` or `missing`. The status table at the end of CONTRACT.md cites the code and tests behind each done or partial status. A build round works down the partial and missing items; a passing local journey does not establish hosted operation, live provider receipt or certification.

The first-release candidate (items 1 to 8 as done or partial) remains the shipped baseline. This target supersedes its earlier "not needed" list: live billing, support, CRM, usage, mail and model connections are now required, under the human-control rules in the contract. Money movement stays outside Buoy (BOUNDARY-001).

## Existing guarantees

1. **HTTP-001 — Authenticated product HTTP** (`done`). Signed out, cross-origin, as a member and as an agent token, call every product route; unauthenticated reads return 401, cross-origin writes 403, members cannot change accounts or approve, and agents can only use the preparation commands.
2. **DATA-001 — Provenanced, validated observations** (`partial`). Create and refresh accounts with valid, negative, impossible-date, missing and stale billing and support observations through the account door; invalid input is refused, missing and stale evidence is named on the account, and the earlier revision remains referenced by earlier work. Repeat with a connected source (CONNECT-001) once it exists.
3. **APPROVAL-001 — Revision-bound human approval** (`done`). Prepare a playbook and a renewal decision, approve twice, complete with and without evidence, refresh the account and attempt approval of a stale proposal; one task and one decision exist, the stale approval returns 409 and the earlier decision keeps its original renewal date.
4. **IMPORT-001 — Reviewed move-in custody** (`done`). Preview a JSON account file, apply with a wrong and then the reviewed fingerprint, replay the same operation key, undo an unchanged import, and attempt undo after preparing work on an imported account; the wrong fingerprint and the dependent undo return 409 and the replay returns the original job.
5. **ERASE-001 — Owner erasure with tombstones** (`done`). As a member, attempt erasure and receive 403; as the owner, erase an imported account with work, then read state, the import journal and history; nothing names the account, and re-creating the same ID returns 409.
6. **RESTORE-001 — Portable installation archives** (`done`). Export an installation with accounts, approved decisions, an agent token and an erased account; restore into an empty copy, then attempt a second restore and a damaged archive; state matches, the agent token is refused, the tombstone holds and both bad restores fail.
7. **SITE-001 — Public run site** (`done`). Build with `rer build --site` and crawl the output through Cloudflare's static-asset server; the homepage reaches `/run` and `/docs`, every guide renders and unknown paths return the 404.
8. **STYLE-001 — One theme at every width** (`partial`). Open setup, sign-in, the account list, an account and the import screen at 1440 and 390 pixels; fonts load from the installation, no content or control is clipped and every form remains usable.

## Accounts, people and the customer record

9. **ACCOUNT-001 — Account hierarchy, people and ownership** (`partial`). Through the account door and API, build a parent with two child accounts and 40 users, assign stakeholders and owners, and read the parent; rollups equal the sum of children, a contact appears on both accounts it belongs to and a member cannot reassign ownership.
10. **ACCOUNT-002 — Unified customer timeline** (`partial`). Connect a CRM, a billing source, a support source and an inbox in a test workspace, generate one record of each kind, and open the account timeline; every entry appears once, in order, with a working source link, and filters return only the chosen kinds.
11. **ACCOUNT-003 — Workspaces, lists and saved views** (`partial`). As a CSM, save a filtered view of at-risk accounts renewing in 90 days, share it, bulk-assign three accounts and enrol them in a playbook; a second CSM sees the shared view, the assignments and enrolments are recorded once each.
12. **ACCOUNT-004 — Custom fields, calculated fields and field history** (`missing`). Add a picklist, a currency field and a formula field "seats used ÷ seats purchased", preview it on ten accounts, save, change an input and read the field history; the formula updates, its explanation shows the inputs and the history records both changes with actor and source.
13. **ACCOUNT-005 — Search** (`missing`). Seed 5,000 accounts, search by a contact email, a Salesforce ID and a phrase from an email body; each result appears within one second and a restricted read-only user does not see accounts outside their scope.

## Connected data

14. **CONNECT-001 — Connector framework, freshness and reconnection** (`partial`). Connect a provider sandbox, run a sync, then revoke the grant and let a run fail; the connection shows the failure and last success, the owner receives a reconnection notice, affected accounts show reduced confidence naming the source, and reconnecting resumes from the cursor without duplicates.
15. **IDENTITY-001 — Identity reconciliation and source authority** (`missing`). Sync overlapping accounts from Salesforce, Stripe and Zendesk where one company appears under two domains; exact ID matches link automatically, the ambiguous pair waits in review, a reviewed merge combines timelines, and undo restores both; an ARR field set to billing-authoritative ignores a conflicting CRM value and shows both.
16. **CRM-001 — Salesforce two-way sync** (`missing`). Against a Salesforce developer org, map fields, sync 1,000 accounts and their opportunities, change an account in each system, and run a writeback; Salesforce shows Buoy's health score and CSM, the conflicting edit follows the authority rule, and the sync log shows API usage under the configured limit.
17. **CRM-002 — HubSpot two-way sync** (`missing`). Against a HubSpot developer account, sync companies, contacts and deals, change a property in each system and write back health; HubSpot shows the Buoy properties, the conflict follows the authority rule and deal stage changes appear on the Buoy timeline.
18. **CRM-003 — Orbit native link** (`missing`). Win a USD deal in Orbit, approve the handoff, and open the new Buoy account with its commitments and owner; replay the handoff and get one account; raise an expansion signal and see the Orbit deal; revoke the link and see a visible failure on both sides.
19. **BILLING-001 — Stripe billing connector** (`missing`). In Stripe test mode, create customers with monthly and annual subscriptions, an upgrade, a downgrade, a failed payment and a cancellation; Buoy's ARR per account equals the Stripe-derived figure, the failed payment raises a risk signal on the right account, and no write call reaches Stripe.
20. **BILLING-002 — Chargebee, Nectar and QuickBooks Online revenue sources** (`missing`). Connect a Chargebee test site, a Nectar installation and a QuickBooks Online sandbox, each with renewals, a downgrade and an overdue invoice; each account's ARR and overdue balance match the source, with the source named on every figure.
21. **BILLING-003 — Subscription financial model** (`partial`). With BILLING-001 data covering twelve months of upgrades, downgrades and one churn, open an account's revenue tab; each month's movement is classified correctly and links to its invoice or subscription change, and a contract-versus-billing mismatch appears in the queue until a person resolves it.
22. **SUPPORT-001 — Zendesk connector** (`missing`). In a Zendesk sandbox, create urgent, breached and solved tickets with CSAT for two organisations; Buoy shows each on the right account timeline, the health measures update on the next run and the source link opens the ticket.
23. **SUPPORT-002 — Intercom and HappyToHelp connectors** (`missing`). With an Intercom test workspace and a HappyToHelp installation, open and resolve conversations for two accounts; Buoy shows them with ratings and HappyToHelp shows the Buoy health and CSM on the customer.
24. **USAGE-001 — Segment and event API ingestion** (`missing`). Send 100,000 Segment events across 50 accounts including duplicates and events for an unknown group; Buoy counts each event once, attributes them to the right users and accounts, and lists the unknown group for mapping.
25. **USAGE-002 — Warehouse sync with Snowflake and BigQuery** (`missing`). Against a Snowflake trial and a BigQuery sandbox, schedule a daily usage query and an export; the metric appears per account with its run history, a broken query shows its error without erasing yesterday's values, and the exported tables contain current health and renewals.
26. **USAGE-003 — Amplitude and Mixpanel connectors** (`missing`). Connect an Amplitude and a Mixpanel test project, sync a feature event and a cohort, and see both attributed to the right accounts.
27. **ADOPT-001 — Adoption and license metrics** (`missing`). With 90 days of synthetic events for 30 accounts, define two features and a seat count; Buoy's active-user, adoption and utilisation figures match an independent calculation from the same events, and a 30% drop in weekly active users is flagged as a trend.
28. **PRODUCT-001 — Product issues and feature requests linked to accounts** (`missing`). Link three accounts to one Jira issue and one to a Penny request, then move the issue to done; the issue shows the three accounts' ARR, each timeline records the status change, and each CSM receives the follow-up task once.

## Health, segments and signals

29. **HEALTH-001 — Configurable, explainable health scores** (`partial`). Build a scorecard with five measures and segment-specific weights, preview it, publish it, then remove a source; every account shows a score with its measure breakdown and sources, history shows the change of version, and the missing source is reweighted or shown as unknown exactly as configured.
30. **HEALTH-002 — CSM sentiment and manual measures** (`missing`). Record a red sentiment with a reason, observe the score change and explanation, then advance past expiry; the measure becomes missing and the explanation says it expired.
31. **SIGNAL-001 — Risk and opportunity alerts** (`partial`). Configure a usage-drop risk and a 90% utilisation upsell signal, generate both, and dismiss one with a reason; each fires once per account, reaches the owner in-app and in Slack, the dismissed one does not re-fire inside its window and the upsell signal can open an expansion opportunity.
32. **SEGMENT-001 — Segments, tiers and lifecycle stages** (`missing`). Define a segment "ARR over $50,000 and health below 60", change an account so it enters and leaves, and run a report filtered by the segment; entry and exit are timestamped, a playbook triggered on entry runs once and the report counts match the rule.

## Lifecycle work

33. **PLAY-001 — Automated playbooks** (`partial`). Build a playbook triggered by health falling below 50 with a task, a wait, a branch on whether usage recovered, an approval-gated email and a Slack post; push three accounts through both branches and a fourth that recovers mid-way; steps run in order, the recovered account exits, no account enrols twice inside the limit and every step is on the timeline.
34. **PLAY-002 — Tasks, reminders and escalation** (`partial`). Create tasks due tomorrow and yesterday, reassign an account, and complete one with an outcome; reminders arrive in-app and by email, the overdue task escalates to the manager, the reassigned task moves and the completed task stores outcome and evidence.
35. **PLAY-003 — Automation run history and failure alerts** (`missing`). Break a webhook step, run a playbook for ten accounts, cross the threshold, fix the step and retry; admins are notified once, the log shows the failures, and retry completes only the failed steps.
36. **PLAY-004 — Outcome tracking for playbooks** (`missing`). Run a playbook for 40 accounts with a defined outcome, let half reach it, and open the playbook report; outcome rate, time to outcome and the unenrolled comparison are shown with their account lists.
37. **ONBOARD-001 — Onboarding projects** (`missing`). Start a project from a template on an Orbit handoff, complete internal tasks, leave a customer task overdue and shift a dependency; dates recalculate, the project shows at risk with the overdue customer task named, and the onboarding report shows time to value per account.
38. **PLAN-001 — Shared success plans and goals** (`missing`). Create a plan with two objectives, one tied to a usage metric and one confirmed by the customer, share it with the champion, and let the metric reach its target; the first objective shows achieved from data, the second waits for the customer's confirmation and health shows the goal measure.
39. **PORTAL-001 — Customer collaboration portal** (`missing`). Invite a champion, complete a customer task and comment from the portal, then attempt to reach another account's portal URL and an internal note; the task and comment appear on the internal timeline and both cross-boundary reads are refused.
40. **REVIEW-001 — Business review preparation and decks** (`missing`). Generate a QBR deck for an account with 90 days of data, edit a slide, export to PPTX and PDF and log the meeting; figures match the account's reports and the follow-up tasks appear on the timeline.

## Push further: agents that carry the job

76. **GOVERN-001 — Agent authority and action ledger** (`partial`). Issue a prepare-only agent and a send-within-policy agent, have each attempt preparation, approval, an in-policy email, an out-of-policy email and a discount; only allowed actions occur, approvals and the discount are refused, the ledger records every attempt, and the kill switch stops both mid-run.
77. **AGENT-001 — Verified risk resolution** (`partial`). Seed an account whose urgent ticket is marked solved but whose feature usage is still zero and whose engineering issue is open; the agent reports the problem as unresolved with citations, proposes a remedy with three owners, chases overdue steps, and marks it resolved only after usage recovers and the customer confirms; the CSM approves the remedy and every message before it is sent.
78. **AGENT-002 — Retention interventions measured against comparable accounts** (`missing`). On a seeded history with a known effect, approve an experiment; the agent's holdout matches on the stated factors, the reported effect falls within the seeded range with an interval, and an experiment that would withhold a contracted service is refused.
79. **AGENT-003 — Evidence-backed renewal readiness** (`partial`). Seed renewals with a departed champion, an unconfirmed goal and an uplift not yet in Stripe; the agent flags each with citations, prepares the timeline for CSM approval and reports the Stripe mismatch until the subscription matches the approved terms.
80. **AGENT-004 — Portfolio-wide causes of risk** (`missing`). Seed 200 accounts where 30 share a defect; the agent reports the cluster with its ARR and citations, files one linked issue after approval, and after the issue closes reports which of the 30 recovered; a CSM scoped to ten accounts sees only their own accounts in the cluster.
81. **AGENT-005 — Scaled-tier digital CSM** (`missing`). Run the agent over 300 scaled accounts for a simulated month with replies of each kind; in-policy outreach is sent, a pricing question and a cancellation threat escalate with summaries, no reply invents a commitment, and every message is in the ledger.
82. **AGENT-006 — Onboarding follow-through** (`missing`). Seed a project with an overdue customer task and a slipped dependency; reminders go to the right people within policy, the proposed new dates wait for CSM approval, and the at-risk alert cites the blocking tasks.
83. **AGENT-007 — Data trust upkeep** (`missing`). Seed a duplicated company, an ARR mismatch and a mis-mapped Segment group; the agent proposes each fix with evidence, applies only what an admin approves, and the affected health scores update with the change noted.
84. **AGENT-008 — Plain-language configuration** (`missing`). Ask for "flag accounts whose weekly active users fall 30% and who have an open urgent ticket"; the draft signal back-tests on seeded history with fire counts and churn capture shown, and nothing is published until the admin approves.

## Honest boundaries

85. **BOUNDARY-001 — Not a billing processor** (`partial`). Inspect the connector scopes requested from Stripe and Chargebee and the API surface; only read scopes are requested and no route moves money.
86. **BOUNDARY-002 — Not the sales CRM, help desk, product analytics or marketing email platform** (`partial`). Follow the documented setup for each sibling and incumbent connector from a fresh installation; each link works without rebuilding that product inside Buoy.
87. **BOUNDARY-003 — No guaranteed prediction** (`partial`). Read any health score, forecast and AI risk signal; each shows its inputs, missing sources and, for closed periods, its accuracy against actual outcomes.
88. **BOUNDARY-004 — No bulk-mail network, consent bypass or autonomous commercial change** (`partial`). Attempt a journey send to a suppressed contact and an agent discount; both are refused and recorded.
89. **BOUNDARY-005 — Certifications are the deployment's** (`missing`). The owner guides list each connector's data flows and the provider terms that govern them.
