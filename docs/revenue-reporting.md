# USD reporting and retention

Open **Revenue** at `/app/revenue`. Reports use person-confirmed recurring terms when present. Otherwise, an explicitly reviewed Stripe customer binding supplies retained subscription reads. Both sources remain separate; IDENTITY-001 owns future cross-source authority and conflict resolution. Invoice totals never substitute for recurring ARR.

Owners and admins publish a named rate table. Fixed corporate rates cover an inclusive 365- or 366-day fiscal year. Daily rates are manually recorded for one UTC date, naming European Central Bank reference rates or Open Exchange Rates; Buoy does not fetch or verify their origin. Each row names `currency`, `from`, `to`, `usdPerUnit` as decimal text and `source`. USD is intrinsically 1. Overlapping periods are refused. Publishing requires the current table revision, retains the author, time and version, and records `revenue.rates-published`. It preserves every native contract, subscription, invoice and payment, restates reporting and invalidates pending account approvals.

`GET /api/buoy/revenue?on=YYYY-MM-DD` shows native ARR and MRR beside USD projections and the rate table. Stripe amounts convert directly from their exact source fractions, before rounding half up to USD cents. ISO currency exponents are respected. Missing rates make USD amounts unavailable. The account revenue panel lists each provider currency separately and links to the native source records. `revenue.usdArrMinor` and `revenue.usdMrrMinor` are explicit USD-cent segment inputs. Native revenue predicates require an exact currency predicate. Failed, disconnected or stale provider sources do not supply current numeric segment inputs.

`GET /api/buoy/revenue/retention?period=quarter&end=2026-06-30` returns native groups, USD amounts, financial movements, separate FX, exact retention numerators and denominators, retained source references and exceptions. Choose `month`, `quarter` or `ttm`. End dates are observed UTC month ends; quarter ends are March, June, September and December. TTM covers twelve calendar months. The starting basis is the UTC day before the period begins.

At each boundary, the last eligible confirmation or actual provider read supplies the source. No historical provider read is manufactured from a subscription created earlier. If an existing subscription has no starting-boundary read, its missing history is an exception rather than an inferred zero. Retained Stripe snapshots include the read and receipt instants, source reference, account revision and original native records. Invoice links open the retained source observation; subscription-change links open its actual before and after images. Invoices without a retained subscription association are explicit exceptions and never add recurring ARR.

Only positive starting ARR enters the retention cohort. NRR divides ending cohort ARR by starting ARR. GRR caps each account's ending ARR at its starting ARR. New and reactivated accounts appear separately and stay outside retention numerators. A reactivation has earlier positive activity, zero starting ARR and positive ending ARR. Missing denominators remain unavailable. Logo counts count distinct accounts.

Expansion, contraction, churn and reactivation use the starting rate at both boundaries. The difference caused by the ending rate is FX alone. Starting ARR plus expansion, minus contraction and churn, plus new business, reactivation and FX equals ending portfolio ARR. A rate edit restates USD projections without rewriting financial sources. Renewals and forecasts use their stated renewal-date rate; retained forecast snapshots keep their original rates.

Optional segment, CSM and tier filters use current membership and assignment. Product filters match either boundary; cohort is the earliest retained contract start or provider subscription creation month. These are explicit current-filter semantics, not historical membership claims. Scoped reports restrict accounts before calculations, totals, source references or exceptions are assembled; their links stay within permitted account doors. Other billing feeds inherit this reporting acceptance when their source clauses ship.

The live qualification journey is `./tests/reporting-sandbox.mjs`: real Stripe test-mode subscriptions and a year of provider invoices in USD, EUR, GBP and CAD, with a EUR upgrade, GBP downgrade, quarter rate change, independent cents and ratios, an unmatched invoice, forecast projections and audited restatement. It claims test-mode behavior only, with neither provider-verified rates nor verified key scope.
