Technical definitions carried from the contract baseline. Target clauses link these rules; descriptions of current doors record their technical limits and confer no additional authority. Agent dispositions are always decided by company policy; historical manual-only slices describe implementation, not target policy.

## DATA-001

USAGE-001 product observations and INAPP-001 view, completion and dismissal receipts attach to accounts only through exact reviewed source/user/group mappings to existing customer people and their current account memberships; they retain source system, source reference, observed and received instants, per-source staleness and account-revision duties. ADOPT-001 seat and per-person license evidence is manual with its own source reference, observation date and threshold; derived adoption metrics are calculations, not observations. CONNECT-001's Stripe test-mode customer-ID staging attributes nothing to an account, is not a connected DATA-001 account observation and is not a billing sync. The owner or an admin sets the threshold for each manual snapshot kind (billing snapshots and support snapshots) from 1 to 365 whole days, 30 by default; those controls do not configure connector thresholds. Evidence is stale when its observation date is more than that many UTC calendar days before today; changing a threshold changes no observation. A currency is any active ISO 4217 code in upper case, or one withdrawn since 2023 (HRK, ANG, BGN, SLL, ZWL) so earlier invoices stay readable. Minor units follow the ISO 4217 exponent (JPY 0, USD 2, KWD 3, CLF 4). Codes without minor units (precious metals, XDR, XSU, XUA, the bond units, XTS and XXX) are refused. Each connected source records its own source system and gets its own threshold before it ships: CONNECT-001 and the connector clauses BILLING-001, BILLING-002, BILLING-005, BILLING-006, BILLING-007, SUPPORT-001 to SUPPORT-005, USAGE-001 to USAGE-004, CRM-001 and CRM-002 inherit this, and CONNECT-001's acceptance repeats this clause's acceptance through a connector.

Entry prepares the existing human-approved playbook proposal; exit or changed source/configuration refuses stale approval. Definition changes advance account revisions before returning. Owner/admin adoption measures now select any exposed numeric metric for an account, any current product user or any current contact, an exact finite-number comparison (lt, lte, eq, gte, gt) or is-missing, and health, alert or both effects. Fractions use ratios, first-value duration uses seconds, and the report’s current/previous UTC windows apply. A related-person measure matches when any one current person matches; absent related people are unknown, never invented matches. A matching health measure adds risk; a required unavailable metric adds unknown unless another source or measure already shows risk. All configured health inputs, manual denominator evidence and installation source coverage remain explained in current health reads and segment/outcome reports. Measures evaluate on reads, source/configuration mutations and the hourly run; projected runway bands retain today’s adoption evidence and do not forecast future usage. Alerts use the existing NOTIFY-001 owner inbox and recipient signal email preferences: current active account owner only, no recipient without an owner, once per matching rule/account/recipient episode; a known non-match, disabled rule or reassignment rearms, while unknown input alone does not prove recovery. Distinct people and events count once across the selected accounts; purchased seats sum across all member accounts and utilization is unknown when any member lacks seat evidence or the total is zero. ## PLAY-002

Reassigning an account moves the open tasks its previous owner held on it; another person's task stays. A reminder goes once within the set number of days before the due date and once on it, and an open task escalates once when the set number of days after its due date has begun, to its owner's named manager or, with none named, to the installation owner. Each goes to one person, in-app and by email through the owner's mail provider, and records only what the provider reported (accepted, refused or unconfirmed, or not sent with no provider or a development mailer); Buoy never claims delivery. Cover is computed, not copied: on every calendar day inside an inclusive away range the cover handles the away person's open tasks and gets their reminders, and the tasks are back with their owner the day after; one person's away periods cannot overlap. Due dates, away ranges and reminder days are calendar dates in the installation's time zone; the reminder run is an hourly Cron Trigger, and the first run at or after 08:00 there each day records that day's reminders and escalations. Tasks link to their account today; PLAN-001 (success plans) and RENEW-001 and RENEW-002 (renewal and expansion opportunities) each link tasks to their records, with these owners, reminders and cover, before they ship.
