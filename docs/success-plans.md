# Internal success plan drafts

Open an account and its **Success plans** section. Create the champion as a customer contact on that account first. A current owner or admin selects the staff owner and customer owner, names the plan and records up to twenty objectives. The account and plan revisions fence every edit; a concurrent change requires reloading and reviewing the current record.

Each objective has a unique id, business objective, UTC due date and kind. A metric objective names an available numeric source metric, finite target and `at-least` or `at-most` comparison. Both bounds are inclusive. Use the **Available source metrics** disclosure to find a key. Usage, received survey and native custom metrics use the same current evidence as the health scorecard. Missing evidence stays unavailable. Metric corrections can return a previously achieved objective to in progress.

A customer statement always waits for authenticated customer confirmation. Internal staff cannot mark it confirmed. Customer sharing, sign-in, invitations and confirmation are future portal work; saving a draft sends no invitation and exposes no customer route.

The health scorecard offers **Current success plan goal attainment**. It divides achieved objectives by all current objectives, including statements still awaiting confirmation. No objectives or any unavailable metric yields unavailable, and the scorecard's chosen missing-value rule applies. Health evidence exposes objective counts and opaque plan revisions, excluding draft text and customer owner details. This current measure does not claim a retained milestone outcome, a causal effect or a comparison cohort.

Account handovers move drafts owned by the outgoing CSM and preserve separately owned drafts. Removing the contact's account membership clears customer ownership. Account erasure removes its plans and version history; customer export includes the current linked ownership records. Installation archives preserve drafts and their history. Restored staff identifiers remain historical assignments and grant no login, permission or replay authority; an active administrator explicitly selects a current staff owner before changing a restored draft.
