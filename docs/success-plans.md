# Success plans and customer confirmation

Open an account and its **Success plans** section. Create the champion as a customer contact on that account first. A current owner or admin selects the staff owner and customer owner, names the plan and records up to twenty objectives. The account and plan revisions fence every edit; a concurrent change requires reloading and reviewing the current record.

Each objective has a unique id, business objective, UTC due date and kind. A metric objective names an available numeric source metric, finite target and `at-least` or `at-most` comparison. Both bounds are inclusive. Use the **Available source metrics** disclosure to find a key. Usage, received survey and native custom metrics use the same current evidence as the health scorecard. Missing evidence stays unavailable. Metric corrections can return a previously achieved objective to in progress.

A customer statement waits until the named customer owner confirms the exact reviewed plan revision through their own native email-link session. Internal staff cannot mark it confirmed. In Customer access, the owner explicitly emails a new customer invitation, waits for email confirmation, then shares the reviewed plan for 1 to 365 days. Sharing itself sends nothing. Give the recipient the customer plan link or the portal home at `/portal`. Each customer sees only their current shared plans, explicitly selected linked tasks, agreed objective status, milestone titles and plan comments. Customer actions record their own confirmation, task completion or comment on the internal timeline. Internal notes, health scores and other accounts stay private.

The health scorecard offers **Current success plan goal attainment**. It divides achieved objectives by all current objectives, including statements still awaiting confirmation. No objectives or any unavailable metric yields unavailable, and the scorecard's chosen missing-value rule applies. Health evidence exposes objective counts and opaque plan revisions, excluding draft text and customer owner details. This current measure does not claim a retained milestone outcome, a causal effect or a comparison cohort.

Account handovers move drafts owned by the outgoing CSM and preserve separately owned drafts. Removing the contact's account membership clears customer ownership. Account erasure removes its plans and version history; customer export includes the current linked ownership records. Installation archives preserve drafts and their history. Restored staff identifiers remain historical assignments and grant no login, permission or replay authority; an active administrator explicitly selects a current staff owner before changing a restored draft.

Under Track objective outcome, select the objective, a comparison segment and a whole UTC-day window. The report freezes the enrolled plans and unambiguous same-objective controls in that segment, with the exact business objective, due date, criterion and customer owner. It accepts numeric metric goals and authenticated customer statements. A changed goal cannot satisfy the original definition. Link the resulting report in the plan editor; linking does not change the frozen objective. The first observation within the window supplies elapsed hours and cohort rates. Completing work alone never supplies success; late evidence remains missed. Open Outcomes for account lists and CSV. Existing healthy and approved-renewal outcome reports remain available too.

Only existing same-account tasks may be linked. Current plans, linked work and milestone evidence are captured in business reviews; renewal records consume current goal evidence. Captured reviews remain historical snapshots.

Customer access is fenced on every request and atomic action. Plan edits, withdrawn contact membership, expiry, revocation, recipient changes and issuing-owner changes stop the old grant. Reactivation does not revive it. Archives retain authenticated customer evidence and captured reviews while excluding grants, recipient authority and sessions. Account and contact erasure withdraw their linked customer records. Staff identifiers in restored plans are historical assignments.
