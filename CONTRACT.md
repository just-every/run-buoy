# Buoy contract

Target edition: 17 October 2026. Buoy is a connected customer success platform for a US head of customer success at a B2B subscription or recurring-service company (roughly 5 to 50 customer-facing people, 100 to 10,000 accounts) replacing Gainsight CS, ChurnZero, Totango or Planhat, with the account workspaces, AI capture and agent execution buyers now see in Vitally and Velaris. Reporting money is USD in integer cents, and subscriptions billed in other currencies keep their native amounts beside it (BILLING-004). Dates and schedules follow US time zones and daylight saving, and the first integrations are the US services those buyers already run: Salesforce (with Service Cloud cases and CPQ), HubSpot, Stripe, Chargebee, Zuora, Zendesk, Intercom, Segment, Snowflake, BigQuery, Amazon S3, Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, SendGrid, Slack and Microsoft Teams.

Buoy does the whole post-sale job those products are bought for. It joins live CRM, billing, support and product-usage data per account and per user. It scores health in a way a CS lead can configure and explain. It turns risk and opportunity signals into automated playbooks and journeys that people and agents carry to a verified outcome. It runs onboarding projects and shared success plans with the customer, captures two-way email, meetings and chat alerts, and keeps a renewal and expansion pipeline whose NRR and GRR reconcile to billing. A buyer of any named incumbent can move their accounts, history, scorecards, playbooks and plans in and switch.

Sibling products keep their markets and are linked, never rebuilt: [Orbit](https://github.com/just-every/run-orbit) is the sales CRM that wins the deal and hands the account over (its INTEGRATE-008); [HappyToHelp](https://github.com/just-every/run-happytohelp) is the help desk; [Nectar](https://github.com/just-every/run-nectar) is subscription billing; [Pencil](https://github.com/just-every/run-pencil) owns general forms and research surveys; [Penny](https://github.com/just-every/run-penny) owns the product feedback board; [Vector](https://github.com/just-every/run-vector) owns issue tracking; [Mango](https://github.com/just-every/run-mango) owns marketing email; [Dial](https://github.com/just-every/run-dial) owns booking links; [Inkwell](https://github.com/just-every/run-inkwell) and [Locket](https://github.com/just-every/run-locket) own signature and contract records; [Nook](https://github.com/just-every/run-nook) is team chat; [Auth](https://github.com/just-every/run-auth) is the workforce identity provider; [Crank](https://github.com/just-every/run-crank) is general workflow automation. Each such seam below is a Buoy integration obligation, not a merged product. No gap in implementation, provider approval or partner agreement reduces this destination; it is recorded as a clause and built.

This is a target contract, not a release verdict. Every named clause states the behavior a buyer gets and its acceptance evidence, the journey or test through a public door that proves it. The status table at the end marks each clause done, partial or missing against the code and tests on `main`; a passing local test does not establish hosted operation, live provider receipt or certification. Existing clause IDs are kept.

HTTP-001 currently exposes three bearer commands, selected in each GOVERN-001 owner grant: `renewals.list`, `playbook.prepare` and `renewal.prepare` on `/api/buoy/commands` only. Successful preparation records the token ID and label in the proposal and account event; each valid scoped request also records its receipt as an attempt, without claiming an outcome; GOVERN-001 enforces the selected command/data-class grant, expiry, rate, USD budget boundary and installation-wide kill switch; future send and update scopes inherit it before shipping. Authorization takes precedence over cookies: malformed, expired or revoked credentials are refused, with no fallback to the cookie session. A valid bearer token may omit Origin or send a foreign Origin, but cannot approve or edit accounts. USAGE-001 has separate per-source server-only intake credentials that grant no agent command, staff session or account read; non-source agent credentials still pass through the agent refusal ledger. Session writes require an exact Origin match and reject `Sec-Fetch-Site: cross-site`. The agent guide documents the separate session-cookie and explicit bearer request paths.

## Product target

Buoy is the customer success platform a US head of customer success buys instead of Gainsight CS, ChurnZero, Totango or Planhat, and picks over Vitally and Velaris: every account's CRM, billing, support and usage data joined and kept fresh, health they can configure and explain, playbooks and journeys that run themselves, onboarding and success plans shared with the customer, and a renewal pipeline whose retention figures reconcile to billing, carried further by agents that verify outcomes under human control.

## Existing guarantees

- **HTTP-001 — Authenticated product HTTP.** Base handles identity and setup; Hono owns authenticated product HTTP handlers. Every data handler checks the current identity and role. A write authenticated with a browser session cookie must carry the installation’s exact Origin and must not carry cross-site Fetch Metadata, so a cross-site page cannot act as the signed-in user. An explicitly scoped bearer token sent in the Authorization header is not sent automatically by a browser and is not subject to the Origin check: CSRF does not apply. Bearer commands must stay within their token’s scopes, refuse out-of-scope actions and audit writes with the agent’s identity. With both a session cookie and a bearer token, only a valid token confers bearer authority; an invalid token must never bypass the session write-origin boundary. Agent credentials reach only the read and preparation commands their scope grants (GOVERN-001). **Acceptance evidence:** Signed out, cross-origin, as a member and as an agent token, call every product route; unauthenticated reads return 401, cross-origin cookie writes return 403, and members cannot change accounts or approve. Send a scoped bearer preparation command without Origin and with a foreign Origin; both succeed and the proposal and audit event identify the agent. Attempt approval and account edits with the bearer token; both return 403, and an unsupported command is refused. Repeat with a session cookie alongside a valid token, then an invalid, expired or revoked token; the valid token keeps only its bearer scope and an invalid token cannot authorize a cross-origin session write.

- **DATA-001 — Provenanced, validated observations.** Every account observation (billing, support, usage, CRM, survey or manual) carries its source system, source reference and observed time. Money is integer minor units of a stated ISO 4217 currency (USD unless BILLING-004 applies) that cannot go negative, dates are real calendar dates, and unsupported fields are refused. Missing and stale evidence stays visible beside the value it qualifies, with a staleness threshold set per source (30 days for manual snapshots by default). No observation is silently overwritten: a refresh creates a new account revision. Today billing and support snapshots are manual, typed in on the account page or moved in from a file under IMPORT-001: their source system is recorded as manual, and their source reference names the record they were copied from. USAGE-001 product observations and INAPP-001 view, completion and dismissal receipts attach to accounts only through exact reviewed source/user/group mappings to existing customer people and their current account memberships; they retain source system, source reference, observed and received instants, per-source staleness and account-revision duties. ADOPT-001 seat and per-person license evidence is manual with its own source reference, observation date and threshold; derived adoption metrics are calculations, not observations. CONNECT-001's Stripe test-mode customer-ID staging attributes nothing to an account, is not a connected DATA-001 account observation and is not a billing sync. The owner or an admin sets the threshold for each manual snapshot kind (billing snapshots and support snapshots) from 1 to 365 whole days, 30 by default; those controls do not configure connector thresholds. Evidence is stale when its observation date is more than that many UTC calendar days before today; changing a threshold changes no observation. A currency is any active ISO 4217 code in upper case, or one withdrawn since 2023 (HRK, ANG, BGN, SLL, ZWL) so earlier invoices stay readable. Minor units follow the ISO 4217 exponent (JPY 0, USD 2, KWD 3, CLF 4). Codes without minor units (precious metals, XDR, XSU, XUA, the bond units, XTS and XXX) are refused. Each connected source records its own source system and gets its own threshold before it ships: CONNECT-001 and the connector clauses BILLING-001, BILLING-002, BILLING-005, BILLING-006, BILLING-007, SUPPORT-001 to SUPPORT-005, USAGE-001 to USAGE-004, CRM-001 and CRM-002 inherit this, and CONNECT-001's acceptance repeats this clause's acceptance through a connector. **Acceptance evidence:** Create and refresh accounts with valid, negative, impossible-date, missing and stale billing and support observations through the account door; invalid input is refused, missing and stale evidence is named on the account, and the earlier revision remains referenced by earlier work. Change a source kind's threshold and the stale evidence it names follows. The connected-source repeat is CONNECT-001's acceptance.

- **APPROVAL-001 — Revision-bound human approval.** A proposal, whether prepared by a person, a playbook or an agent, snapshots the account revision it was based on. Approval re-checks that revision in one atomic D1 batch, records the approving person and time, and creates exactly one task, decision or approved action. A replayed approval changes nothing. Completion needs separate evidence. A source refresh invalidates pending approvals and never rewrites earlier decisions. This is the gate every sensitive step in PLAY-001, EMAIL-002, RENEW-001 and the agent clauses uses. **Acceptance evidence:** Prepare a playbook and a renewal decision, approve twice, complete with and without evidence, refresh the account and attempt approval of a stale proposal; one task and one decision exist, the stale approval returns 409 and the earlier decision keeps its original renewal date.

- **IMPORT-001 — Reviewed move-in custody.** Every import, including each incumbent migration in MIGRATE-001 to MIGRATE-005, runs through the single Base import door: original-byte upload, no-write plan, the exact reviewed fingerprint, idempotent apply, native reconciliation and a journal with strict undo that survives restart. The owner alone imports at `/app/imports` (also `/app/import`); earlier admin jobs remain readable and undoable by the owner, without a second importer. Business imports require an enabled door and a signed action-bound grant; self-hosted installs start disabled. RER-provisioned installs enable only with `RER_IMPORT_DOOR=on` after owner claim and heal on later owner paths without reopening an owner-disabled door. Revoked signing keys stay revoked. The import page alone permits same-origin static scripts and connections; its mutations require the current owner session and exact Origin. The current format is a JSON account snapshot (up to 50 records and 256 KB); unsupported records are reported, credential removals are disclosed, imported accounts are inert, and installation archives remain separate. A later snapshot uses explicit catch-up; absent source records are retained with that explanation, never deleted. Undo refuses accounts edited since import or with dependent work. Erasure withdraws original bytes and door snapshots together with native rows and the portable restore checkpoint. **Acceptance evidence:** Through the migrated Worker, upload and plan a JSON account file; native reads are unchanged. Apply with a wrong fingerprint (403), then the reviewed fingerprint; replay returns the same job, reconciliation matches native records, and undo removes unchanged accounts. An owner edit and dependent work each refuse undo (409). Anonymous, member, revoked-session and cross-origin mutations fail; disabled or revoked Business grants fail; owner undo works with Business disabled. Claim with and without the provisioning flag, disable and revisit the owner path, and re-enable after key revocation; only the provisioning opt-in opens initially, an explicit disable stays closed and revoked keys stay revoked. Erase an imported account and read its public job/journal plus door reconciliation; retained source values disappear, the journal inventories door custody, reconciliation returns snapshot withdrawn, and native replay is refused.

- **ERASE-001 — Owner erasure with tombstones.** Owner erasure, confirmed by typing the account name, atomically removes the account, its work, decisions and native and import custody; permanent target and source tombstones prevent replay into that installation. Copies held by connected systems are listed on the erasure receipt for the owner to action (ERASE-002); Buoy does not claim to delete them. **Acceptance evidence:** As a member, attempt erasure and receive 403; as the owner, erase an imported account with work, then read state, the import journal and history; nothing names the account, and re-creating the same ID returns 409.

- **RESTORE-001 — Portable installation archives.** Local RER archives carry domain data and import custody into an empty destination. New owner authentication is required; credentials, agent tokens, connector secrets, the outbox and settings are excluded. Corrupt and populated restores fail. Archives scale with SCALE-001. **Acceptance evidence:** Export an installation with accounts, approved decisions, an agent token and an erased account; restore into an empty copy, then attempt a second restore and a damaged archive; state matches, the agent token is refused, the tombstone holds and both bad restores fail.

- **SITE-001 — Public run site.** The static run site contains the product homepage, `/run`, Markdown owner guides and a 404. It reads no installation data and links only public routes. Installed `/` keeps the shared Lobby. Run-site deployment stays gated off until its release gate. **Acceptance evidence:** Build with `rer build --site` and crawl the output through Cloudflare's static-asset server; the homepage reaches `/run` and `/docs`, every guide renders and unknown paths return the 404.

- **STYLE-001 — One theme at every width.** The run site, Lobby, sign-in, setup and product share one theme file and Buoy's self-hosted faces: Inter for the wordmark, headings and text (headings in an ultra-heavy, tightly tracked display weight that keeps clear word spaces, the second line in the plum-to-turquoise gradient), Caveat only for hand-drawn notes and IBM Plex Mono for commands; white grid paper, near-black ink and the mark's plum, with red for the one failure, green for recovery and amber for Watch, always with a text label; and the smiling life-ring mark with tuned 16 and 32 pixel favicons. Real forms, role boundaries, checklist content and evidence remain available at desktop and phone widths. **Acceptance evidence:** Open the homepage, `/run`, `/docs`, setup, sign-in, the account list, an account and the import screen at 1440 and 390 pixels; fonts load from the installation, the mark and the 16 and 32 pixel favicons show, no content or control is clipped and every form remains usable.

## Target clauses

### Accounts, people and the customer record

- **ACCOUNT-001 — Account hierarchy, people and ownership.** Parent organizations, accounts and end users form one hierarchy, with rollups of ARR, users and health from children to parents. Each account holds stakeholders with roles (champion, executive sponsor, economic buyer, admin, billing contact, end user), the assigned CSM, account executive, executive sponsor and support owner, a lifecycle stage, a tier and custom fields. Contacts can belong to several accounts. An account can also hold relationships (a product line, subscription or business unit under one company, as Gainsight models them), each with its own health scorecard, CSM, renewal and timeline, rolling up to the company. New hierarchy and relationship fields become SEGMENT-001 rule inputs before shipping. Before these record kinds ship, they inherit ACCOUNT-004 in full: the same text, number, currency, date, picklist, multi-select, boolean and calculated field types; arithmetic, conditions, UTC date differences, dependencies and hierarchical or portfolio rollup formulas; no-write previews against ten real accounts and their related records over the full portfolio; per-result input and missing/error explanations; actor, UTC time, old/new value and source history with source-owned refresh and recalculation; and typed SEGMENT-001 inputs with same-person conditions. Customer-person rollups count each distinct person once across memberships. Their public HTTP and native 1440/390 journeys must assert all these points before shipping. **Acceptance evidence:** Through the account door and API, build a parent with two child accounts and 40 users, assign stakeholders and owners, add two product-line relationships with different CSMs to one account, and read the parent; rollups equal the sum of children, each relationship keeps its own health and CSM and rolls up to its company, a contact appears on both accounts it belongs to and a member cannot reassign ownership.

- **ACCOUNT-002 — Unified customer timeline.** Each account and contact has one chronological timeline of emails, meetings, calls, notes, support tickets, invoices and payments, usage milestones, survey responses, playbook steps, tasks, approvals and renewal events. Every entry links to its source record and system and can be filtered by kind, person and source. Notes support @mentions and attachments. **Acceptance evidence:** Connect a CRM, a billing source, a support source and an inbox in a test workspace, generate one record of each kind, and open the account timeline; every entry appears once, in order, with a working source link, and filters return only the chosen kinds.

- **ACCOUNT-003 — Workspaces, lists and saved views.** Each CSM has a home showing today's tasks, alerts, renewals and meetings for their book. Account and user lists support filters on any field or metric, chosen columns, sorting, bulk actions (assign, enroll in a playbook, add to segment), and personal or shared saved views. A manager can see any CSM's book. **Acceptance evidence:** As a CSM, open the home, then save a filtered view of at-risk accounts renewing in 90 days, share it, bulk-assign three accounts and enroll them in a playbook; the home lists exactly that CSM's tasks due today, open alerts, renewals within 90 days and today's customer meetings, a manager opening that CSM's home sees the same list, a second CSM sees the shared view, and the assignments and enrollments are recorded once each.

- **ACCOUNT-004 — Custom fields, calculated fields and field history.** Admins add typed custom fields to accounts, users, contacts and opportunities, and calculated fields with a plain formula language (arithmetic, conditions, rollups, date differences) that previews against real accounts before saving and explains each result. Every field change keeps who, when, old and new value and source. Custom objects (ACCOUNT-006) use the same field types, formulas and history. New custom and calculated fields become typed SEGMENT-001 rule inputs before shipping. Today every existing account, contact and product-user field capability conforms: owner/admin-defined text, number, currency, date, picklist, multi-select, boolean and calculated fields, account-value editing and history, real-account preview and typed SEGMENT-001 inputs. USAGE-001 carries separate contact/user definitions and values, explicit account memberships, previews against ten real accounts and customer records, calculations over the full customer-person portfolio, source-owned old/new history and typed related-person SEGMENT-001 inputs. The same person must satisfy an all-rule’s conditions for one related kind, including the currency and amount. Customer-person rollups include all current customer people once each, irrespective of how many account memberships each has. ACCOUNT-001 adds these same obligations for relationships and hierarchical rollups; RENEW-001 and RENEW-002 add opportunities; ACCOUNT-006 adds custom objects before those record kinds ship. Native access and approval rules remain the account rules already in force. Formulas use finite numeric arithmetic with ordinary precedence, same-type comparisons and lazy if(condition, then, else), UTC calendar-day differences, and explicit portfolio sum/average/count rollups. Missing inputs and division by zero produce no value with an explanation; sum/average require every current account to have a numeric input and count counts present values. Currency retains its ISO minor-unit amount and currency code with no implicit conversion. Custom numeric segment comparisons accept fractions, booleans use 1/0 and multi-selects use JSON array text; monetary segment comparisons name both currency and amount. Omitted manual keys clear their values. A type cannot be changed in place, and removing an in-use choice is refused. Preview changes no persisted definition, value or history, and displays ten real accounts while evaluating the full portfolio. Every changed manual or calculated value records actor ID, UTC instant, old/new value and source; dependency changes recalculate and advance the affected account revision before a successful write returns. Account erasure removes its field values and history, and current rollups use the remaining accounts. **Acceptance evidence:** Add a picklist, a currency field and a formula field "seats used ÷ seats purchased", preview it on ten accounts, save, change an input and read the field history; the formula updates, its explanation shows the inputs and the history records both changes with actor and source.

- **ACCOUNT-005 — Search.** One search finds accounts, contacts, users, timeline entries, tasks, plans and opportunities by name, domain, email, external ID and text, respecting the reader's permissions. The current partial implementation is a staff-session HTTP search over native accounts, customer contacts/users and approved tasks, including their current custom/calculated text values and recorded domain/email/external-ID text. It matches a literal substring, folds ASCII case only, and pages live records by kind and native ID; percent and underscore are not wildcards. It grants the existing account/customer read authority only and preserves the full acceptance below as remaining work. **Acceptance evidence:** Seed 5,000 accounts, search by a contact email, a Salesforce ID and a phrase from an email body; each result appears within one second and a restricted read-only user does not see accounts outside their scope.

- **ACCOUNT-006 — Custom objects.** Admins define custom objects for the records a CS team keeps beside accounts, such as product instances, implementations, license entitlements, usage rollups, or Salesforce objects such as CPQ subscriptions and Assets. Each object has typed fields: text, number, currency (BILLING-004), date, picklist, multi-select and boolean, plus lookups to account, relationship, user, contact, opportunity or another custom object. Each object also has a required external ID, and field history as in ACCOUNT-004. Account and relationship pages show each object's records in a section the admin configures, and the timeline records their creation and changes. Custom-object fields work wherever a standard field does: calculated fields and rollups (ACCOUNT-004), health measures (HEALTH-001), signals (SIGNAL-001), segments (SEGMENT-001), playbook triggers and branches (PLAY-001), lists and saved views (ACCOUNT-003), reports and dashboards (REPORT-002), search (ACCOUNT-005), and the API with bulk upsert by external ID (API-001). Records load from file sources (USAGE-004), warehouse queries (USAGE-002), CRM custom sync jobs (CRM-001 for any Salesforce standard or custom object, CRM-002 for HubSpot custom objects) and incumbent migrations (MIGRATE-001 to MIGRATE-005). ROLE-001 scope applies: a record is readable only by those who can read the account or relationship it looks up to, and admins can mark a field commercial so the field rules cover it. Deleting an object or field that a scorecard, signal, playbook or report uses is refused, and the refusal names those dependents. Erasing an account or contact (ERASE-001, ERASE-002) removes the records that look up to it. An installation holds at least 50 custom objects of up to 200 fields each, within SCALE-001. Custom-object fields and relationships become SEGMENT-001 rule inputs with matching rule acceptance before shipping. Before these record kinds ship, they inherit ACCOUNT-004 in full: the same text, number, currency, date, picklist, multi-select, boolean and calculated field types; arithmetic, conditions, UTC date differences, dependencies and hierarchical or portfolio rollup formulas; no-write previews against ten real accounts and their related records over the full portfolio; per-result input and missing/error explanations; actor, UTC time, old/new value and source history with source-owned refresh and recalculation; and typed SEGMENT-001 inputs with same-person conditions. Customer-person rollups count each distinct person once across memberships. Their public HTTP and native 1440/390 journeys must assert all these points before shipping. **Acceptance evidence:** An admin creates a "Product instance" object with a lookup to account and three typed fields (environment picklist, active seats, last deploy date). They load 200 records from an S3 file (USAGE-004) and 50 from a Salesforce custom object through a CRM-001 custom job. Every record lands on the right account with no duplicate by external ID. A health measure "share of instances deployed in the last 30 days" and a report of active seats by account return values that match an independent calculation from the source data. A CSM outside an account's scope gets none of its rows through the UI, API, search or export. Deleting the object while the measure uses it is refused, naming the scorecard. A Gainsight export with one custom object migrates (MIGRATE-001) with record counts and field types matching.

### Connected data

- **CONNECT-001 — Connector framework, freshness and reconnection.** Every connector authenticates with OAuth or a scoped key held encrypted (SECURE-002), syncs on webhooks where the provider offers them and on a schedule otherwise, and records per-run counts, cursor, errors and last success. A connections page shows each source's freshness; a disconnected or failing source notifies its owner and admins, rechecks itself, and lowers the confidence of every health score, alert and forecast that depends on it. Nothing is presented as current when its source is stale. Every connector's observations meet DATA-001: they carry the connector's source system, source reference and observed time, pass the same validation and go stale after the connector's own threshold. Scheduled file sources (USAGE-004) have an expected arrival window: a file that is late, missing or rejected counts as a failed run. Every connector connection, credential change, reconnection and disconnection records an AUDIT-001 entry (actor kind and id, time, action, opaque subject) before this clause ships. **Acceptance evidence:** Connect a provider sandbox, run a sync, then revoke the grant and let a run fail; the connection shows the failure and last success, the owner receives a reconnection notice, affected accounts show reduced confidence naming the source, and reconnecting resumes from the cursor without duplicates. Repeat DATA-001's acceptance through the connector: its observations name the connector as their source system, invalid provider records are refused, and stale evidence follows the connector's threshold.

- **IDENTITY-001 — Identity reconciliation and source authority.** Accounts, contacts and users are matched across systems by external IDs (Salesforce, HubSpot, Stripe, Zendesk, Intercom, Segment group IDs), domain and email, with a review queue for ambiguous matches and an undoable merge. Admins choose, per field, which system is authoritative; writes from a non-authoritative source are recorded but do not overwrite. This includes revenue: an admin chooses whether CRM-contracted ARR (BILLING-005) or billed ARR (BILLING-001, BILLING-002, BILLING-006, BILLING-007) leads, per account segment, and differences between them go to BILLING-003's reconciliation queue. **Acceptance evidence:** Sync overlapping accounts from Salesforce, Stripe and Zendesk where one company appears under two domains; exact ID matches link automatically, the ambiguous pair waits in review, a reviewed merge combines timelines, and undo restores both; an ARR field set to billing-authoritative ignores a conflicting CRM value and shows both.

- **CRM-001 — Salesforce two-way sync.** Accounts, contacts, users, opportunities (including renewal and expansion opportunities), owners and mapped custom fields sync from Salesforce. Through the same connection Buoy also reads Cases (SUPPORT-004), and opportunity products, Contracts and Salesforce CPQ Subscription and Asset records as a revenue source (BILLING-005). Admin-defined custom sync jobs sync any other Salesforce standard or custom object into a Buoy custom object (ACCOUNT-006), with a field mapping, an external-ID key and a schedule. Buoy writes back health score, health color, CSM, lifecycle stage, renewal risk and selected fields to mapped Salesforce fields. It also writes the CS touches an admin chooses by kind (CSM emails, meetings, calls, business reviews and completed tasks) to Salesforce as Activities (Task or Event records) on the account and contact, so account executives see them in Salesforce; each is written once and updated if it changes in Buoy. Field mapping, sync direction per field, conflict handling and API-limit pacing are visible and editable. A Buoy Lightning Web Component on Salesforce account, opportunity and case pages shows health with its reasons, CSM, renewal date, open risks and recent timeline within the viewer's ROLE-001 scope. Taking over fields an incumbent wrote is CRM-004. **Acceptance evidence:** Against a Salesforce developer org, map fields, sync 1,000 accounts and their opportunities, change an account in each system, run a writeback, run a custom job from a custom object, and log a meeting in Buoy. Salesforce shows Buoy's health score and CSM, and the Buoy component on the account page shows the same health and CSM. The conflicting edit follows the authority rule. The custom object's records arrive in their ACCOUNT-006 object on the right accounts. The meeting appears once as a Salesforce Event on the account. The sync log shows API usage under the configured limit.

- **CRM-002 — HubSpot two-way sync.** Companies, contacts, deals, owners and custom properties sync from HubSpot, with the same writeback of health, CSM, stage and renewal risk to HubSpot properties, per-field direction and conflict rules as CRM-001. Through the same connection Buoy reads Service Hub tickets (SUPPORT-004) and deal line items as a revenue source (BILLING-005). Custom sync jobs bring HubSpot custom objects into Buoy custom objects (ACCOUNT-006), and chosen CS touches are written to HubSpot as engagements (notes, meetings, calls and tasks) on the company and contact. **Acceptance evidence:** Against a HubSpot developer account, sync companies, contacts and deals, change a property in each system, write back health, run a custom-object job and log a Buoy meeting. HubSpot shows the Buoy properties, and the conflict follows the authority rule. Deal stage changes appear on the Buoy timeline. The custom object's records arrive in their ACCOUNT-006 object, and the meeting appears once as a HubSpot meeting engagement on the company.

- **CRM-003 — Orbit native link.** Buoy receives Orbit's post-sale handoff (Orbit INTEGRATE-008): account and contact identity, purchased products, commercial commitments, sales evidence references and the receiving owner, under a stable handoff ID that Buoy accepts or refuses with an actionable reason and reconciles on replay. Permitted account context (health, CSM, renewal date, expansion signals) flows back to Orbit. CS-qualified expansion leads become Orbit deals (RENEW-002). **Acceptance evidence:** Win a USD deal in Orbit, approve the handoff, and open the new Buoy account with its commitments and owner; replay the handoff and get one account; raise an expansion signal and see the Orbit deal; revoke the link and see a visible failure on both sides.

- **CRM-004 — Salesforce field handover at cutover.** A buyer leaving Gainsight (or ChurnZero, Totango, Planhat or Vitally) keeps the Salesforce reports, dashboards, list views and flows that read the fields the incumbent was writing, such as health score, health color, CSM, lifecycle stage and renewal risk. A cutover plan lists each such field, its current writer (from Salesforce field history), the Buoy value mapped to it with any scale or picklist translation (for example a 0 to 100 score to the incumbent's color labels), and the Salesforce reports and dashboards that reference it (from the Salesforce Analytics API). During the parallel run (MIGRATE-006) Buoy writes only to shadow fields; at cutover an admin switches each field to Buoy writes in one reviewed step and can switch a field back. **Acceptance evidence:** In a Salesforce developer org with three fields Gainsight populated and a report and a dashboard that use them, run the cutover plan, switch two fields and run a writeback; the plan lists all three fields with the report and dashboard, Buoy's values arrive in the two switched fields on the mapped scale, the report and dashboard return values for every account, and the field left on its old writer is untouched.

### Lifecycle work

- **PLAY-001 — Automated playbooks.** Playbooks start on a signal, segment entry, lifecycle stage change, a date relative to renewal, contract start or onboarding milestone, a webhook, or by hand. Steps create tasks, send or draft emails (EMAIL-001), post to Slack or Teams, wait for a time or condition, branch on data, update fields, start another playbook, call a webhook (or a Crank flow) or pause for approval (APPROVAL-001). Each playbook has entry and exit criteria, re-enrollment limits, owners and per-step due dates, and stops when its exit criteria are met. Every playbook publish records an AUDIT-001 entry (actor kind and id, time, action, opaque subject) before this clause ships. - **PLAY-002 — Tasks, reminders and escalation.** Tasks (Gainsight calls them CTAs) have an owner, due date, priority, type and linked account, plan or opportunity. Owners get reminders before and on the due date; overdue tasks escalate to the owner's manager after a configured delay; tasks move to the new owner when an account is reassigned or the owner is away. Completing a task records an outcome reason and evidence. Priority is low, medium (the default), high or urgent; type is risk (the default), onboarding, adoption, renewal, expansion or general. A task's owner is the one named when it is prepared, else the account's owner at approval, else the approving person. Until ROLE-001 and TEAM-001 add CS managers and teams, the owner or an admin sets each account's owner, each person's manager, away periods with their named cover, the reminder and escalation delays (1 to 30 whole days each, default 1) and the installation's time zone for task days (an IANA name; UTC until one is set); members cannot, including for themselves. Reassigning an account moves the open tasks its previous owner held on it; another person's task stays. A reminder goes once within the set number of days before the due date and once on it, and an open task escalates once when the set number of days after its due date has begun, to its owner's named manager or, with none named, to the installation owner. Each goes to one person, in-app and by email through the owner's mail provider, and records only what the provider reported (accepted, refused or unconfirmed, or not sent with no provider or a development mailer); Buoy never claims delivery. Cover is computed, not copied: on every calendar day inside an inclusive away range the cover handles the away person's open tasks and gets their reminders, and the tasks are back with their owner the day after; one person's away periods cannot overlap. Due dates, away ranges and reminder days are calendar dates in the installation's time zone; the reminder run is an hourly Cron Trigger, and the first run at or after 08:00 there each day records that day's reminders and escalations. Tasks link to their account today; PLAN-001 (success plans) and RENEW-001 and RENEW-002 (renewal and expansion opportunities) each link tasks to their records, with these owners, reminders and cover, before they ship. **Acceptance evidence:** Create tasks due tomorrow and yesterday, reassign an account, mark a CSM away with a named cover, and complete one with an outcome; reminders arrive in-app and by email, the overdue task escalates to the manager, the reassigned task moves, the away CSM's due tasks move to the cover for the away period and return after it, and the completed task stores outcome and evidence.

- **PLAY-004 — Outcome tracking for playbooks.** Each playbook records which enrolled accounts reached its intended outcome (health recovered, milestone reached, renewal closed) within a window, with completion rates, time to outcome and comparison to unenrolled accounts in the same segment. Today every existing manual or segment-entry task playbook can be tracked: an owner/admin defines healthy evidence or a new approved renewed decision, sets a 1–365 whole UTC-day window and attaches one existing approved task per enrolled account. The outcome must be unsatisfied at baseline; accounts must currently belong to the chosen segment. This single start operation freezes both the enrolled accounts and the other unsatisfied members of the same segment as the comparison group. Later segment changes do not rewrite either cohort. The window starts at tracking creation and includes its deadline; an outcome is the first satisfied evidence observed on a request, after a source/action change or on the hourly run, not an inferred transition time. Renewed evidence must be a new approved decision at or after tracking start; healthy evidence must be the account's actual current healthy band. The first timestamp and source revision or approved decision citation persist even if the account later regresses. A window closes durably on its first run after the deadline, unresolved accounts show missed, and later recovery never counts. Rates are reached divided by the current retained cohort size (zero for an empty cohort); every account lists its elapsed UTC hours, and the mean includes only reached accounts. No observed outcomes is explicit rather than zero time. Displayed hours and percentages round to at most two decimal places (nearest, ties away from zero); CSV retains calculation precision. Owner erasure removes the subject and its evidence from both lists and denominators. ## Push further: agents that carry the job

Agents act with GOVERN-001's scoped authority on the data named in each clause. People keep control of concessions, commitments, external promises and anything that changes money or a contract.

- **GOVERN-001 — Agent authority and action ledger.** Each agent has an owner-issued, expiring, revocable credential with named scopes (read, prepare, send within policy, update fields), a USD spend budget, rate limits and the data classes it may read. Agents may prepare anything in scope but cannot approve their own proposals; external sends follow EMAIL-002 policy and approval settings; concessions, discounts, credits, contract changes and product commitments always need a person with that approval right. Every agent read, proposal and action lands in an action ledger, and a kill switch stops all agents at once. The DONE boundary covers all current commands: `renewals.list`, `playbook.prepare` and `renewal.prepare`. The owner issues a nonempty named command grant, a nonempty list of account terms, billing snapshots, support snapshots and CSM sentiment data classes, a 1–365 whole-day expiry (7 by default), a per-UTC-minute request cap (1–10,000; 60 by default) and a nonnegative integer-cent USD budget. Expiry is the issue instant plus that many 24-hour days. Minute windows are UTC epoch minutes; every authenticated request, including a refusal or malformed body, consumes one atomic reservation, and a 429 names the seconds until the next minute in Retry-After. Partial-class reads omit excluded fields and all derived health, segments and outlook; preparation needs all four classes because its immutable review snapshot contains them. Existing credentials retain their prior three commands, all four classes and original seven-day expiry; they can be revoked and deliberately reissued. Every authenticated attempt has a separate durable succeeded/refused outcome, without storing a credential or request payload in the action ledger.
